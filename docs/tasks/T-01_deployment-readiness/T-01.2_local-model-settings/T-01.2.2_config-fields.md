## Specification

- `model`: `env.get("STT_MODEL", "small.en").strip() or "small.en"`; `compute_type`: trimmed, lower-cased, blank = `int8`; `threads`: blank = `2`, otherwise a whole number `>= 0`, else `ValueError` whose message names `STT_THREADS` and the offending text (`0` means "let ctranslate2 choose"; document that). No other validation (T-01.2.1 question 3).
- Keep every existing field and default; `SttConfig("openai", "en", "")` must still equal `SttConfig.from_env({})` (an existing test relies on it).
- Do not read a new variable anywhere else, and do not change the engines or the composition root (T-01.2.3).
- Do not log the value of `OPENAI_API_KEY`; the new settings may be logged.
